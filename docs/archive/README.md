# Archive — parked, kept, not live

Work that was real, is finished or deliberately stopped, and should not be picked up again
without a decision. It sits here rather than in `docs/` so the live tree shows what is live.

**Nothing here is deleted and nothing here is wrong on purpose.** The repository's rule is that
corrections stay visible; several of these documents are the record of judgements that were
made and, in two cases, reversed.

## The one exception

The `M.MMRM` implementation is *prepared* for upstream in
[`../contribution-mmrm.md`](../contribution-mmrm.md) (LG, 2026-09-19) and is **not** parked.
It is working code rather than a critique, and only LG files it. Preparing it is not a licence
to prepare anything else.

## What is not here

The thirteen findings stay in [`../findings/`](../findings/README.md). They are by-products
rather than the product, but three of them — F06, F12 and F13 — are the clearest statements of
what the framework can and cannot express, they are cited from the README, and they are the raw
material for the "what to look out for" sections of the guide. Archiving them would bury the
most teachable thing the traverse produced.
